# Figure legends

Figure files keep the filenames produced by the generator scripts in
`scripts/figures/`. The article figure number each corresponds to is given below.

### `Fig5_PRLR_alleles` — Figure 1
Model of the *PRLR* gene (BTA20) with the positions of the truncating alleles
SLICK1 (c.1382del), SLICK2 (c.1489C>T) and the candidate c.1393C>A; the functional
consequence of every coding site; and the allele frequency of each focal site per
reference panel/breed. Generated by `scripts/figures/fig_prlr_alleles.py` (and, for
the gene model plus Tables, `scripts/figures/fig_gene_and_tables.py`).

### `Fig6_class_ancestry` — Figure 2
Ancestral reference panel assigned to each carrier haplotype, stratified by
functional class of site (synonymous, non-synonymous, introns, whole gene).
Generated by `scripts/figures/fig_class_ancestry.py` from the output of
`scripts/ancestry_haplotype/gene_ancestry_by_class.py`.

### `Fig7_network` — Figure 3
Haplotype network of the *PRLR* coding sites (nodes = distinct coding haplotypes;
node composition = ancestral panels; distances = Hamming between unique types).
Generated by `scripts/ancestry_haplotype/haplotype_network.py`.

### `Fig2_ranking` — Figure 4
Apparent West African (African taurine) ancestry across all populations under the
supervised ADMIXTURE (K=3) estimator, ranked. Generated by
`scripts/figures/fig_genomic_ancestry.py`.

### `Fig3_controls` — Figure 5
The two controls: composition of the lineage-paired control and of the
zebu-without-African-founder composites, plus the supervised ADMIXTURE vs.
unsupervised sNMF comparison. Generated by `scripts/figures/fig_genomic_ancestry.py`.

### `Fig_PRLR_truncation_map` — Figure 6
Where each slick allele truncates the prolactin receptor: the 581-residue bovine
receptor (UniProt Q28172), with SLICK1 (frameshift near residue 461), SLICK3
(Ser465*) and SLICK2 (Arg497*) mapped onto the extracellular, transmembrane and
cytoplasmic regions. All three remove the distal STAT5-docking tyrosine while
retaining the Box1 JAK2 anchor. Generated by
`scripts/figures/fig_prlr_truncation_map.py`.
